Support

Need help, found a bug or have an idea? Email YOUR EMAIL and include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working. I'll do my best to reply within a few days.

My characters disappeared.

Your cast is saved only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes your characters, and they can't be recovered. They do come back if you restore the whole iPhone from a backup.

I bought the Full Version on another phone.

Open Settings in the game, tap Full Version, then Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

I can't hear anything.

If your phone is on silent, the game starts quiet and tells you so. Tap Sound On, or switch sound back on in the game's Settings.

The voices sound robotic.

You can download better voices for free on your iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English. The "Enhanced" ones sound best. Then pick one for a character on their Voice & Lines page (the ✎ on Your Cast).

How do I record a line in my own voice?

Open a character's Voice & Lines page, add a line, and tap the microphone. Allow microphone access when your iPhone asks.

I'm stuck on a level.

Fail the same level three times in a row and the results screen offers Skip Level for one ad. You can come back for the stars any time.

Achievements aren't showing up.

Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in your iPhone's Settings.

Can I start over?

Yes. Tap Start Over on the level map. Your levels and stars reset, but your characters and your unlocks are kept.
