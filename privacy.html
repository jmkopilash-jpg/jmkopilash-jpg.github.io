Privacy Policy

Last updated: DATE

Splatharsis is a game made by YOUR NAME. This page explains what happens to your information when you play it.

The short version: Everything you make in the game stays on your phone. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some information about your device to show and measure those ads.

What stays on your phone

  • Your cast. The characters you make are stored only on your device, along with their names, faces, outfits, lines and chosen voices. They're never uploaded anywhere, and the ads never see them.
  • Voice recordings. If you record lines in your own voice, the game uses the microphone only while you're recording. The recordings are saved only on your device and never leave it.
  • Progress and settings. Your levels, stars, unlocks and settings are stored only on your device.

If you delete the app, all of this is deleted with it. We can't recover it, because we never have a copy.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Splatharsis shows ads between some levels, and an optional ad you can watch for extra throws or to skip a level. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure them, Google may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier, your IP address and approximate location, how you interact with the ads, and crash and performance data. Google's use of this information is covered by Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Your choices about ads

  • Tracking. The game asks once whether it may use your device's advertising identifier ("Allow tracking"). If you say no, you'll still see ads, but they won't be personalised. You can change this at any time in your iPhone's Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Europe, the UK and Switzerland. Where the law requires it, Google's consent message asks for your choices before any ads are shown. You can change them later in the game's Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • The Full Version. A one-time purchase turns off the ads between levels.

Purchases

Buying the Full Version is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Apple's Game Center, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple so they can appear on leaderboards. Apple handles this under its own privacy policy.

Sharing

The Share button makes a picture of your result and opens your phone's share sheet. Nothing is sent unless you choose where to send it. The picture never includes your characters' faces or names.

Ratings

The game may show Apple's built-in "rate this app" prompt. Apple runs that prompt, and we don't see who rates or what they rate.

No accounts

There's no sign-up, and we don't collect your name, email or contacts.

Children

Splatharsis isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
